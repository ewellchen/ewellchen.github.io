I am an Associate Professor and Master's Supervisor at Yunnan University. My group develops data-efficient methods for quantum machine learning, single-photon and computational imaging, and industrial signal processing.

I received my B.Eng. (2018) and Ph.D. in Control Science and Engineering (2023) from Zhejiang University. My Ph.D. supervisor was Prof. Yu Pan. I then worked on computational imaging as a postdoctoral researcher at Heriot-Watt University in Edinburgh. I lead projects supported by the NSFC and Yunnan Province; my CV provides more detail.

My work has appeared in Phys. Rev. A, Phys. Rev. Applied, Opt. Express, Quantum Sci. Technol., IEEE Trans. Cybern., IEEE Trans. Instrum. Meas., IEEE Trans. Artif. Intell., IEEE Sensors J., Pattern Recognit., Neurocomputing, Eng. Appl. Artif. Intell., and Expert Syst. Appl., as well as ICASSP, EUSIPCO, and IFAC World Congress.

I have reviewed for IEEE Trans. Neural Netw. Learn. Syst., IEEE Trans. Image Process., IEEE Trans. Comput. Imaging, IEEE Trans. Circuits Syst. Video Technol., IEEE Trans. Syst. Man Cybern. Syst., IEEE Trans. Cybern., and IEEE Trans. Instrum. Meas., along with journals in optics, quantum science, imaging, and AI. The full journal list is in my CV and ORCID review record. I hold the IOP Trusted Reviewer certification and received the 2025 IEEE TIM Outstanding Reviewer honor.

Join the team

Students and collaborators are welcome. We warmly invite you to contact us about research collaborations in quantum machine learning, computational imaging, and industrial signal processing. Building on these strengths, we plan to combine large language models with domain knowledge to develop domain-specific foundation models for scientific and industrial applications.

Our team works closely with research groups at Zhejiang University, the National University of Singapore, the University of Edinburgh, and the University of Technology Sydney.

Oct 2026 Our paper on Hamiltonian-induced error-channel control was published in Physical Review A. It studies how quantum state-transfer errors can be shaped for controlled convergence.
Oct 2026 Our sensor-aware single-photon imaging study appeared in Optics Express. It brings sensing physics into deep learning-based image reconstruction.
