<div class="about-intro">
  <p>I am an <strong>Associate Professor</strong> and <strong>Master's Supervisor</strong> at Yunnan University. My group develops data-efficient methods for <strong class="research-accent research-accent-quantum">quantum machine learning</strong>, <strong class="research-accent research-accent-imaging">single-photon and computational imaging</strong>, and <strong class="research-accent research-accent-industrial">industrial signal processing</strong>.</p>

  <p>I received my B.Eng. (2018) and Ph.D. in Control Science and Engineering (2023) from Zhejiang University. My Ph.D. supervisor was <a class="advisor-link" href="https://person.zju.edu.cn/ypan" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Yu Pan</a>. I then worked on computational imaging as a postdoctoral researcher at Heriot-Watt University in Edinburgh. I lead projects supported by the NSFC and Yunnan Province; my <a href="{{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}">CV</a> provides more detail.</p>

  <p>My work has appeared in <strong>Phys. Rev. A</strong>, <strong>Phys. Rev. Applied</strong>, <strong>Opt. Express</strong>, <strong>Quantum Sci. Technol.</strong>, <strong>IEEE Trans. Cybern.</strong>, <strong>IEEE Trans. Instrum. Meas.</strong>, <strong>IEEE Trans. Artif. Intell.</strong>, <strong>IEEE Sensors J.</strong>, <strong>Pattern Recognit.</strong>, <strong>Neurocomputing</strong>, <strong>Eng. Appl. Artif. Intell.</strong>, and <strong>Expert Syst. Appl.</strong>, as well as ICASSP, EUSIPCO, and IFAC World Congress.</p>

  <p>I have reviewed for <strong>IEEE Trans. Neural Netw. Learn. Syst.</strong>, <strong>IEEE Trans. Image Process.</strong>, <strong>IEEE Trans. Comput. Imaging</strong>, <strong>IEEE Trans. Circuits Syst. Video Technol.</strong>, <strong>IEEE Trans. Syst. Man Cybern. Syst.</strong>, <strong>IEEE Trans. Cybern.</strong>, and <strong>IEEE Trans. Instrum. Meas.</strong>, along with journals in optics, quantum science, imaging, and AI. The full journal list is in my <a href="{{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}">CV</a> and <a href="https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5739-2429" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ORCID review record</a>. I hold the IOP Trusted Reviewer certification and received the 2025 IEEE TIM Outstanding Reviewer honor.</p>
